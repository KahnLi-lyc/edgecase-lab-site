Frequently asked questions

Does EdgeCase Lab require an account?

No. The app has no account system and works offline.

Is my test data uploaded?

No. Inputs and generated content are processed on your device. Content leaves the app only when you explicitly copy or share it.

Where are presets stored?

Preset rules and recent rules are stored locally on your device. Generated values and inspected content are not saved as part of recent rules.

How do I remove local data?

Delete individual presets from the Presets workspace. Uninstalling EdgeCase Lab removes its locally stored app data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.

Which devices are supported?

EdgeCase Lab supports iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

Before reporting a problem

  • Confirm your device is running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
  • Include the app version, device model, and the steps that reproduce the issue.
  • Use fictional or redacted examples. Never post credentials, tokens, personal data, or production records.

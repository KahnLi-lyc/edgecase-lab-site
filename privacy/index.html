Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

EdgeCase Lab does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app works without an account or network connection.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information, usage analytics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or other device data. EdgeCase Lab does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Information you enter or generate

Text, JSON, timestamps, generated test data, and other content you use in EdgeCase Lab are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to a cloud service.

When you choose Copy or Share, iOS handles the content according to your action and the destination you select. EdgeCase Lab does not receive a copy.

Data stored on your device

EdgeCase Lab may save your appearance and output preferences, reusable preset rules, and a limited list of recent rules locally on your device. Recent rules do not include generated values or inspected content.

You can delete saved presets in the app. Removing EdgeCase Lab from your device removes its locally stored app data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior controlled by you and Apple.

Third-party services

The app does not connect to third-party services. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and does not add cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. GitHub may process basic request information needed to deliver the website under its own privacy terms.

Children's privacy

Because we do not collect personal data, EdgeCase Lab does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the change is released. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the EdgeCase Lab support page.
